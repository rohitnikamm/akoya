Pill height and Mac options

Three things to look at: where the pill sits when your taskbar is hidden, the Mac's missing options added to the Windows tray panel, and the messages you only see when something goes wrong.

1. Pill height when the taskbar auto-hides

Right now the pill sits 18 px above whatever space the taskbar leaves free. With auto-hide, that's the very bottom edge of the screen, so it hugs the edge, and the taskbar covers it if your mouse touches the bottom.

  • Jelly pop: the pill appears as a small circle, stretches out past its full width, and springs back. It's done in 0.42 s, so it never holds up your first words (recording starts the moment you press, whatever the animation is doing).
  • Processing colour: I'd keep it blue-white. Akoya already uses yellow for "something's off" (the mic row that isn't detected, "isn't hearing you" in setup), so a yellow sweep on every dictation would read like a warning.
  • Red while recording: I'd make it a switch, off by default, same as the Mac (section 3). Red is a familiar "recording" signal, but in the pill it's also the error colour, so it's better as your choice than the default.

  • Waveform: Pixel or Solid, and Red while recording: same as the Mac, applied to the pill. Only the waveform turns red; the glow stays blue so it never looks like an error.
  • Hide when idle is left out: on Windows it's already how things work. The tray icon sits tucked behind ⌃ and the pill only shows while you talk.
  • Hotkey choices differ on purpose: Windows has Right Alt, Right Ctrl and the Copilot key instead of Option, Command and Control.

4. Messages you'll only see when something goes wrong

This is why you didn't see them: a normal dictation only shows the waveform and then "Pasted". Each of these comes up in one specific situation.

Copied. Press Ctrl+V

You're typing into an app running as administrator. Windows blocks other apps from pasting into it, so Akoya copies your words and you paste them yourself.

!Couldn't paste

Another app is holding the clipboard. Your words are kept in the tray panel under Last dictation, with Copy.

!Microphone not detected

Your chosen mic is unplugged. Nothing is recorded, and the Microphone row in the tray panel turns yellow.

!Turn on your mic in Settings

Windows' microphone privacy switch is off. The tray panel gets an Open Settings button.

Getting the speech model ready…

You hold the key during the first-time download. The tray panel shows the progress.

!Speech model failed

The download or loading failed. The tray panel gets a Retry button.

No mic found
Plug one in. It will show up here.

Setup, when the PC has no microphone at all. The list fills in by itself when you plug one in.

Didn't catch that. Try again.

Setup's Try it step, if it heard silence. Your previous take stays on screen.

Model download failedRetry

Setup's Try it step, if the download breaks (Wi-Fi drops, for example).
